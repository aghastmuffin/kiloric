## 3. Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information

Under CPRA, consumers have the right to limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information to that which is necessary to perform the services reasonably expected by an average consumer.

kiloric collects sensitive personal information (body metrics, age, biological sex, health targets) **strictly and exclusively** to calculate BMR, TDEE, recommended daily calories, and macronutrient breakdowns for your personal tracking. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for any secondary purposes. Users can toggle and confirm this restriction at any time via the in-app CPRA Privacy Portal.

## 4. How to Submit a Verified Consumer Privacy Request

You or your authorized agent may submit a privacy request using any of the following methods:

1. **In-App CPRA Privacy Portal**:
   Open kiloric mobile app -> Navigate to `You -> Privacy Portal` or `Account -> CPRA & CCPA Privacy Portal`.
   - Request instant **Data Export** (JSON format download/copy).
   - Initiate **Account & Data Deletion**.
   - Manage **Do Not Sell/Share** and **Limit Sensitive Data Use** preferences.
   - Submit formal privacy requests.

2. **Web CPRA Privacy Portal**:
   Visit `https://kiloric.co/privacy-portal` (or open `website/index.html`). Submit an interactive request form for Access, Deletion, Correction, Opt-Out, or Sensitive Data Limitation.

3. **Email Request**:
   Send an email to `data_privacy@kiloric.co` with `kiloric - Data Privacy Request` in the subject line. Include the account email address and details of your request.

## 5. Request Verification & Authorized Agents

- **Identity Verification**: Signed-in users submitting through the app portal are authenticated automatically. For web or email requests, we verify identity by sending a confirmation code or verification email to the account email address registered with kiloric.
- **Authorized Agents**: An authorized agent submitting a request on a consumer's behalf must provide proof of written permission signed by the consumer and verify their own identity with us.
