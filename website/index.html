01 — How it works

Simple as counting to three.

Getting started takes under two minutes. No quiz funnels, no upsells, no account gymnastics.

01

Set your target

Enter your baseline metrics. kiloric calculates calorie and macro goals with the standard Mifflin-St Jeor formula — nothing proprietary, nothing hidden.

02

Log your meals

Search the catalog, scan a barcode, or reuse a recent meal. Adjust the portion and save with a tap — servings scale against your targets automatically.

03

Stay on track

Remaining calories and macro balances update live as you move through the day. Missed days are recorded honestly as unlogged — never as fake zero-calorie diets.

02 — Privacy

Your data stays yours.

You shouldn't have to trade personal health habits for a simple nutrition log. kiloric stores only what you log — and never sells your profile.

Zero data sold or shared

No third-party advertisers, data brokers, or analytics resellers. Ever.

Strict account isolation

Health metrics are encrypted in transit and scoped to your account with bearer-token sessions.

Export or delete, instantly

Download your complete data or erase your account permanently from settings — no tickets, no waiting.

CCPA & CPRA for everyone

Full California-grade privacy rights applied to every user, wherever you live.
