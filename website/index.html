Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Most nutrition trackers drown you in paywalls, social feeds, and streak pressure. kiloric keeps it focused: fast lookup, clear macros, and complete privacy.

Fast Daily Logging
Search the catalog or scan a barcode to log meals in seconds. Servings scale automatically against your targets.
Macro Breakdown
Track protein, carbs, and fat alongside daily calories. Visual bars show where you stand at a glance.
Privacy First
Your nutrition data belongs to you. No tracking pixels, no ad brokers, and full export or deletion whenever you want.
Works Offline
Cached queries and local state keep your log responsive even when cell service drops in the grocery aisle or gym.
Food Database
Built on open nutrition data with community contributions so you can submit missing foods and keep the catalog accurate.
Progress Insights
Review 7-day and 30-day intake patterns. Missing days are recorded honestly as unlogged days, not false zero-calorie diets.

Privacy

Your data stays yours.

You shouldn't have to trade your personal health habits for a simple nutrition log. kiloric stores only what you log, protects your account with strict bearer tokens, and never sells your profile.

  • Zero data sold or shared with third-party advertisers
  • Health metrics encrypted and strictly isolated to your account
  • Instant data export and permanent account deletion in settings
  • Full CCPA & CPRA compliance applied to every user
