Part 1: Privacy Policy
1. Introduction & Overview
Welcome to kiloric ("Service", "App"), operated by Taeson NORTH AMERICA Ltd. Liability Co. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information and health/wellness data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we store and protect it, and the rights you have concerning your data under applicable data protection laws (CCPA/CPRA).
2. Information We Collect
We collect information directly from you when you register, complete onboarding, configure your settings, log nutrition/water data, or interact with our features:
•
Account & Authentication Information: Email address, name/display name, and password credentials protected by the Kiloric API.
•
Profile & Demographic Information: Biological sex, date of birth / age, self-reported race, country of residence, whether you reside in any country in Asia, and account roles. kiloric is not offered to residents of the European Union, Russia, or Vietnam.
•
Physical & Health Metrics: Height, current body weight, target body weight, weight log history, and calculated metrics using strictly user-provided data (such as Basal Metabolic Rate and Total Daily Energy Expenditure).
•
Fitness, Diet & Nutrition Goals: Fitness goals (e.g., weight loss, maintenance, muscle gain), goal pace, calculated and custom caloric targets, macronutrient targets (protein, carbohydrates, fats), water intake targets, and dietary style preferences.
•
Daily Tracking & Activity Data: Meal logs, food entries, timestamped nutritional intake (calories, macros, micronutrients), and water consumption records.
•
User Contributed Content & Media: EXPLICIT USER CONSENT: Custom food items, nutrition labels, and uploaded food images submitted for food logging or community contribution.
•
App Preferences & Technical Metadata: Display units (Metric / Imperial), theme preferences (Dark / Light / System), typography settings, search preferences, client device identifiers, operating system version, crash reports, and application logs.
3. How We Use Your Information
We process your data for the following purposes:
•
Service Delivery & Core Functionality: To calculate personalized nutritional plans, daily energy budgets, macro breakdowns, and track progress toward your goals.
•
Account Management & Authentication: To authenticate your identity, secure your account, and manage your user profile.
•
Personalization: To remember your theme, typography, unit system preferences, and search modes across sessions.
•
Food Database Improvements: To parse and verify community-contributed food items and nutrition labels.
•
Service Reliability & Security: To monitor performance, detect abuse, troubleshoot crashes, and enforce terms of service.
•
Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable statutory obligations and protect our legal rights.
We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal health data to data brokers or third-party advertisers.
4. Third-Party Service Providers & Sub-processors
We share data only with trusted service providers necessary to operate the App:
•
Backend & Database Hosting (Kiloric API): User accounts, database records, and contributed media are handled by our dedicated API infrastructure at api.kiloric.co.
•
Nutrition & Food Database APIs: External food search APIs (such as Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central) to look up product nutrition facts. Queries sent to these APIs contain product search strings or barcodes, not your personal health profile.
•
Crash Reporting & Telemetry (e.g., Sentry): Error and diagnostic logs to diagnose app crashes and ensure stability.
5. Your Rights & How to Request Data Updates or Deletion
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data: (otherwise, the kiloric project team will still honor requests to delete or export your data).
•
Right of Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you in a structured, machine-readable format.
•
Right to Rectification / Update: Request corrections or updates to inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
•
Right to Erasure / Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated health, meal, and profile records.
•
Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Object to or restrict certain data processing activities.
•
Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw previously granted consent for specific processing operations at any time.
CPRA & CCPA Privacy Portal
Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents and all kiloric users globally have the right to access, export, correct, or delete personal data, as well as confirm Do Not Sell/Share and Sensitive Data Limitation preferences.
How to Submit a Privacy Request
Signed-in users can start deletion or export directly from our Privacy Portal or Account settings. Account access is removed immediately and the remaining deletion process is completed within 30 days. For exports, updates, or help:
1.
Send an email to data_privacy@kiloric.co.
2.
Include kiloric in the subject line (for example, "kiloric - Data Export Request").
3.
Body Details: Please include the email address associated with your kiloric account so we can verify your identity before processing the request.

Part 2: Data Retention Policy
1. Retention Principles
Taeson NORTH AMERICA Ltd. Liability Co. retains your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain active service functionality, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.
2. Retention Schedules by Data Category
Account & Profile Data

Physical Metrics & Target Records

90 days rolling retention
Automatically purged after 90 days
Crash & Error Telemetry
Diagnostic error data
30–90 days

Inactive Accounts

24 months of total inactivity
Notified via email prior to deletion; purged if no response
3. Deletion and Purge Procedures
When an account deletion request is processed:
1.
Primary Database Records: Profile, goal, weight, meal, water, planning, workout, task, and contribution records associated with the user ID are deleted by the Kiloric API.
2.
File Storage: Images and files uploaded by the user are removed with the account.
3.
Authentication Record: The authentication record and active refresh tokens are deleted when the account deletion request succeeds.
4.
Backups: Residual data in automated system backups will be overwritten in accordance with standard backup rotation cycles (not exceeding 30 days).
Part 3: Data Security
We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal and nutritional data:
•
Encryption in Transit: All network communication is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS).
•
Encryption at Rest: User credentials and sensitive database records are encrypted at rest.
•
Access Control: Access to backend servers and customer data is restricted to authorized personnel on a strict need-to-know basis with multi-factor authentication (MFA).
Part 4: Children's Privacy
kiloric is an adult-only service and requires every user to be at least 18 years old. Eligibility is checked before account creation and whenever the saved date of birth changes. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an ineligible person created an account, we will block access and promptly delete the associated information.
