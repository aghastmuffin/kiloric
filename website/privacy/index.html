Notice at Collection

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), we provide notice regarding the categories of personal data collected, business purposes, and retention rules.

kiloric DOES NOT SELL OR SHARE personal information or sensitive personal health metrics with third parties or data brokers.

Your California Statutory Rights

Right to Know & Access

Request details about categories and specific pieces of personal data collected about you, or download a structured JSON export.

Right to Erasure / Deletion

Request permanent deletion of your account and associated physical metrics, meal logs, and profile records.

Right to Correct

Request correction or update of inaccurate personal data recorded in your profile or settings.

Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Share

Direct us not to sell or share your data for cross-context behavioral advertising (kiloric never sells user data).

Right to Limit Sensitive Data

Restrict processing of sensitive personal health information strictly to core application service functions.

Right to Non-Discrimination

You will never be discriminated against, charged different rates, or denied service for exercising privacy rights.
